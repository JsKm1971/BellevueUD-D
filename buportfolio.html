Flip Cards

Hover over or use the Tab key to focus on each card and reveal the answer.

Q1. What are the two primary components of risk?
Answer: Likelihood and impact.
Q2. What is a favorable uncertain outcome called?
Answer: An opportunity.
Q3. What generally happens to risk as likelihood or impact increases?
Answer: Risk increases.
Q4. What are the three basic risk-tolerance attitudes?
Answer: Risk-averse, risk-neutral, and risk-seeking.
Q5. Should effective risk management be proactive or reactive?
Answer: Proactive.
Q6. What are the three basic conditions for decision-making discussed in risk management?
Answer: Certainty, risk, and uncertainty.
Q7. Under which decision condition are probabilities of outcomes known or reasonably estimated?
Answer: Risk.
Q8. Under which decision condition are the probabilities of outcomes not known?
Answer: Uncertainty.
